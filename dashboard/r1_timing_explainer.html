📊 R1 Timing — When is a Top/Bottom CONFIRMED?

Understanding the difference between daily close confirmation and intraday R2 entries

⚡ The Core Rule (R1)

A TOP or BOTTOM is confirmed only at end of trading day (15:30).
Entry/exit via R2 fires during the trading day — but uses only already-confirmed levels.

❓ At 11:00 AM on Day 4 — we have Day 1, 2, 3 closes. Day 5 doesn't exist yet.
Can we say "Day 3 is a TOP"?
R1 needs: close[i] > close[i-1] AND close[i] >= close[i+1]
→ Day 3 = 1370 > 1360 ✓ AND Day 3 = 1370 >= Day4(1365) ✗
Second condition FAILS — Day 3 cannot be confirmed as TOP yet.
✅ Only at 15:30 on Day 5 — when Day 5 closes at 1360:
Day 3 = 1370 > Day 2 = 1360 ✓ AND Day 3 = 1370 >= Day 5 = 1360 ✓
BOTH conditions pass → Day 3 is NOW a confirmed TOP.
📌 Key insight: Even though the TOP is on Day 3, it is only confirmed at the close of Day 5.
During Day 4 trading, Day 3 is NOT a confirmed level for R2 entries.
Only levels confirmed on previous days' closes can be used for intraday R2 entries.

🎯 Bottom Line

During any trading day, R2 uses levels confirmed by yesterday's close or earlier.
Today's candle cannot confirm a new level — it needs tomorrow's close too.
So a Top/Bottom identified on Day N becomes actionable only from Day N+2's intraday trading.
