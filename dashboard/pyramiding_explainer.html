📐 Pyramiding Rules — Complete Explanation

⚡ Core Pyramiding Rules

  1. Every new entry uses the same lot size as the initial entry
  2. When a new pyramid entry fires, initial trade's SL → moves to new bottom (break-even protection)
  3. New pyramid entry → Day 1 SL = Rule 6
  4. From Day 2 of new entry onwards → all units share same unified SL (Rules 3 & 4)
  5. Every entry always follows R1, R2, R6 → then R3/R4 → independently from their own Day 1

❓ My Question: What if Entry 3 fires BEFORE Entry 2 reaches Day 2?

Scenario: Entry 2 fires on Day 4. Entry 2 is Day 1.
Then on Day 5 (still Day 1 of Entry 2), a new bottom B3 forms and Entry 3 fires via R2.
Question: Does Entry 2's Day 1 end on Day 5? Or does it wait for Entry 3 to also reach Day 2?
Answer: Each entry is independent.

On Day 5:
→ Entry 2 is now Day 2 → its SL follows Rules 3 & 4
→ Entry 3 fires as Day 1 → its SL follows Rule 6
→ Entry 1 → managed by Rules 3 & 4 (already Day 2+)

On Day 6:
→ Entry 3 is now Day 2 → ALL THREE units share ONE unified SL
→ Managed together by Rules 3 & 4
